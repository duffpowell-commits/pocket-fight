# Wildmade: pitch (repo pocket-fight)

**One line:** Say what monster you want, watch it hatch, then see what it can do.

Status: **one-week side test**, started Nov 23, 2026. Lethemoor stays Duff's active game. Verdict
due **Nov 30, 2026**. The test answers one question: *is making a monster fun enough to do twice?*

## Three pillars (every feature must serve one, or it's cut)
1. **You made it.** The monster on screen is yours: words, taps or a roll, and every part stays editable.
2. **Looks are free.** No look changes a fight. Only the move you pick does. Nobody is punished for taste.
3. **It does something.** A monster you made gets to act right away: it hops, uses its move, fights.

## Core loop
- Every 30 seconds: change a part, or type a few words, and see the monster change.
- Every 5 minutes: finish a monster, name it, take it into a short fight, come back and tweak or start another.
- Every session: leave with a monster you'd show someone.

## Like these, but
- **Creature Crafter** (parts editor, online worlds): Wildmade builds from a sentence and plays offline.
- **MonsterCrafter** (block-by-block pet + battles): no grind before your monster looks like your idea.
- **Create Your Own Monster** (kids' part picker + minigames): same instant fun, plus words and a real fight.
Market note (Nov 23, light check): part-picker monster makers exist on phones and are mostly kids'
apps or online grinders. "Type it and it hatches" is the part none of them lead with.

## Platform and price
Android APK first (dev release + in-app updates). Price is undecided until the test passes.

## Kill criteria (written up front)
- By Nov 30, Duff hasn't made at least 5 monsters on his phone because he wanted to.
- A tester (Brittany or Maddi) doesn't make a second monster without being asked.
- Median fun under 6/10 from the playtest sheet after one round of fixes.
- The slice needs more than this week.
Hit one: say so, then pivot a pillar, shrink, or park.
