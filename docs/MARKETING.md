# 3 Eyed Fox Studios: Marketing and release playbook

Owner: 3 Eyed Fox Studios. Applies to every game derived from godot-game-starter.
Last reviewed: 2026-11-23. Marketing budget: $0 until explicitly approved.

## Source of truth and safeguards
This document is a **plan**, not evidence of published or scheduled posts. Never label a post scheduled, published, or verified without a platform URL, account, timestamp/time zone, and confirmation. Never create accounts, publish, or spend money without the owner's approval. Keep credentials in platform integrations, never in Git. Verify game names, claims, footage rights, and release links before publication. Don't post unapproved character art or spoilers.

## Studio positioning
Brand: **3 Eyed Fox Studios**, distressed gold tattoo-inspired three-eyed fox, gritty humor, memorable characters, indie games with personality. Use approved logo assets only; preserve the mascot's ear jewelry and styling. Consistent publisher credits and studio splash on game builds. Distinguish studio posts from individual game announcements.

## Content pipeline
1. Pick a verified playable feature or approved concept; write a 1-sentence hook.
2. Capture 9:16, 1080x1920 footage, with readable safe-area captions; include sound, licensed/owned music, and accessible captions.
3. Make a 15–35 second cut: 0–2 sec hook, 3–25 sec gameplay/payoff, final 3–5 sec studio/game name and one honest CTA.
4. QA: audio present, no personal info, no false feature claims, no unapproved assets, link works, correct publisher branding.
5. Owner approves asset, caption, channel, and publication date; record approval.
6. Schedule via approved platform-native scheduler or authorized integration. Store confirmation IDs/links.
7. Check published URL, engagement, click-throughs, downloads and retention at 24h and 7d; revise next batch.

## Tracking schema
Maintain a private or appropriately public content calendar with columns:
content_id, game, platform, asset_path, caption, CTA, owner_approval, status(draft/approved/scheduled/published/failed), scheduled_at_timezone, published_at, platform_post_id, post_url, impressions, views, average_watch_time, engagements, link_clicks, installs_or_plays, notes.
Avoid committing account tokens or private analytics. An empty scheduled_at means **not scheduled**.
